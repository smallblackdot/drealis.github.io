What this project is

Applying to college can feel overwhelming. We are peers who have been through (or are going through) the same process. We share plain-language explanations, timelines, and tips so you can ask general questions—about how things work, what terms mean, and what to think about next—and leave with a clearer picture. We are not a replacement for your school counselor or official financial aid offices; we point you in the right direction and help you feel less lost.
